About us

An Owner-Managed House

No manager, no agent. You talk to us about rent.

No beds free right now. Tell us when you are looking and we will put you first when one opens.

The name

Prajna means wisdom, insight and discernment, which is the feeling we wanted the house to have. Ten beds across four twin-sharing and two single rooms. The full ground floor for living - kitchen. Dining hall, TV lounge, reception.

How we run it

Rent conversations happen directly with us. We don't take commission from you, so there's no agent fee. The one thing not in the rent is electricity, and it's metered. Problems get fixed by the people who own the place.

Who it is for

Working professionals in the Moshi-Chakan belt - shift workers. People new to Pune, people saving. Most residents walk or ride a two-wheeler to a gate on the corridor. Ask for the distance to yours and we’ll tell you straight.

What we hold to

  • Clean rooms, and common spaces we keep clean
  • Straight pricing
  • CCTV at the gate, and we answer when you call
  • You can see the room before you pay us anything
