Neighbourhood

Around the house

Where to shop, how to get to work. Where to eat, what to do on a Sunday.

No beds free right now. Tell us when you are looking and we will put you first when one opens.

Daily needs

  • Moshi vegetable market - fresh produce at local prices
  • Moshi APMC market - big wholesale yard, go early
  • Supermarkets and kirana shops along the Spine Road and Balaji Nagar stretch
  • Hardware, cycle and two-wheeler shops across the MIDC belt

Commute from the gate

  • Factory gates of Moshi MIDC - walkable to many. Two-wheeler minutes to the rest.
  • Chakan corridor along the Nashik highway - the region's biggest auto-manufacturing strip
  • PMPML bus stops on the main road - connections across PCMC and into Pune city.
  • Moshi market and the APMC yard - daily shopping

Food nearby

  • Mess-style eateries around the MIDC gates - full meals built for shift workers.
  • Street food stands toward Moshi village and the market corners
  • Sweet shops and snack stops along the highway for the evening chai run.
  • The house’s own kitchen and dining hall, steps from your room

Health and essentials

  • Clinics and pharmacies inside the estate and along the main roads
  • Multi-speciality hospitals within a short ride on the PCMC side
  • PCMC civic services and police points reachable quickly from the belt
  • Full address on the map and in writing

Weekends within reach

  • Alandi - a temple town worth a day rather than an errand
  • Sinhagad fort - the classic Pune day trek, mornings are best
  • Lonavala and Khandala - ghat views and monsoon waterfalls, an hour-plus away
  • Pune city proper - Shaniwar Wada, Dagdusheth Ganpati, the old city lanes

The work belt itself

The corridor carries some of India’s biggest auto and engineering plants plus hundreds of component factories. Job-hop within the belt without changing your home.
