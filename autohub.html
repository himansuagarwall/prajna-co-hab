Want an account? Pit login is optional

The Pit is open to everyone. Anyone can read the board, ask a question, post and reply without logging in. An account is only for people who want one: it gives you a name people recognise, a place to find your posts again, and a way to hear when someone replies.

With an account you can

  • Be recognised by name on your posts and replies
  • Save posts you want to come back to
  • See your own posting history in one place
  • Get a notification when someone replies to you (concept, by email)

Without an account you can (always)

  • Read every post, and search and filter the board
  • Ask a question and use the listing templates
  • Write a post and reply, just by email

How an account works: email only

  1. Request an account by email. Fill in the form below. It opens your email app addressed to srijna2b@gmail.com with your name, area, vehicle and why you want an account already filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there.
  2. The owner verifies and invites you. We check the request, then reply by email with your invite. There is no password page, no app and no login system on this site. This is a request flow, not fake authentication.
  3. Post from your invited email address. That is how the owner recognises account holders. Every post is still read before it appears.

No WhatsApp, no phone numbers published, no fake accounts, and no payment through this site. If anyone asks you to pay to post, it is not us. Skipping this section costs you nothing: the board, the ask form and replies stay open on this page.

Community code (applies to everyone)
  • Straight answers only. One post per item. No repeated posts and no shop promotions dressed up as questions.
  • No phone-number dumps and no links to agents. Replies come through us by email.
  • Share what you actually did: what was fixed, what it cost you, which office or shop in Moshi, Bhosari, Chakan or Pimpri, and roughly when.
  • Disagree with the advice, not the person. Keep it useful for the next rider.
  • Verify in person, in daylight, before you pay anything. The owner moderates and has the final word.

How the Pit stays trustworthy

  • Pit rules: straight answers only, one post per item, disagree with the advice - not the person.
  • No spam: no repeated posts, no phone-number dumps, no shop promotions in the question form.
  • Verify in person: inspect the bike or part yourself, in daylight, before you pay anything.
  • No payment via this site: Prajna never collects money for posts, and you should never pay in advance.
  • Replies come through us: contact happens by email after we’ve read the post. We have the final word.
