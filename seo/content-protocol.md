# Content Protocol — Prajna blog/guides

Consistency anchor: /content/voice-and-tone.md and /content/site-copy.md.

1. One post, one job. Title answers a real question.
2. First 3 lines = the direct answer. No delay.
3. Tight list — 10-15 words per line, one point per line.
4. Close with one action: "Ask us about moving in."
5. No filler words, no banned-verb list word. Banned: delve, surge, paramount, vital, deploy, synthesize, tapestry, bolster, synergy, realm, holistic, revolutionize, game-changing, at its core, testament, navigate the complex, multi-faceted, pivotal moment.
6. Real numbers, no hidden rounding. Two-wheeler parking, covered.
7. Alt-text captions factual. No invented "owner took this."
8. End each post with one email CTA.
