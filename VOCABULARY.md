# Prajna Co-hab — Vocabulary (core)

One voice for the whole site. Short sentences. Active voice.
Maximum 12 words per sentence. No emojis. No em-dashes.
Indian, friendly, formal tone. Facts and prices never change here.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning on this site | Prajna fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Pit** | Community board for auto and bike help. | Open to all. No fee. No login needed. |
| **in-room** | Inside your room. Bed and storage stay inside. | Double in-room means two beds in one room. |
| **lock-in** | Minimum stay. You stay at least this long. | 3 months at Prajna Co-hab. |
| **notice** | Advance warning before you leave. | 1 month after lock-in ends. |

## Rules for new copy

- Write one idea per sentence.
- Say the actor first. "We reply by email." Not "Replies come by email."
- Use these four terms only with the meanings above.
- First use on a page adds the short meaning in brackets.
- Prices come from `config.js` only. Never type a price in prose.
- Twin-sharing means two beds in one room. Private means one bed.
- Dinner means dinner only. Breakfast and lunch are never included.
