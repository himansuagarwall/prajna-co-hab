Questions

Asked often

Is food included in the rent?

Dinner only, and it is optional. Breakfast and lunch are not included at either price; the kitchen is yours if you want to cook. Dinner adds ₹2,800 a month.

Is WiFi available?

Yes, high-speed WiFi is included in all rooms.

What is the minimum stay?

Three months to start with, then one month of notice. The deposit is refundable.

Is the location safe for women?

CCTV coverage, secure access, and a house manager on premises. The neighbourhood is well-lit and family-friendly.

Can I see the room before booking?

Yes. Email us and we will set a same-day or next-day visit, whichever suits your shift.
