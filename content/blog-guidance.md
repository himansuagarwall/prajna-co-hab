# Blog & Guide Rewrite Discipline

Short. Informative. Human. The blog is the site's largest words asset, so it must earn attention instead of spamming it.

## Structure (one post, one job)
- Title answers a question the reader already asks.
- First 3 lines: the direct answer. Never a delay.
- Then a tight list of what, why, how. One point per line, 10–15 words max.
- Close with one action: "Ask us about moving in."

## Voice
- Matured, warm, direct. No jargon. No filler words.
- Real numbers, no rounding that hides. "Two-wheeler parking, covered."
- No "in today's fast-paced world" or any banned-verb list word.

## Protocol-ready
- Every post must fit a single clear answer in a sentence.
- Heading hierarchy mirrors the answer, not decoration.
- Image captions are factual. No invented "owner took this."

## Example rewrite
**Bad:** "Navigating the complex, multi-faceted reality of co-living requires pivotal finesse."  
**Good:** "Living in a co-living room. Here's what you should check before you move in."
