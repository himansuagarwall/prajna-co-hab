# Voice & Tone — Prajna Co-hab

Owner voice. Written for co-living seekers on the Bhosari Industrial Estate, Balaji Nagar, Pune 411026.

## The person behind the words
A grown woman who runs a house. Warm. Firm. Plain. Not a salesperson, not a brochure. Say it like a friend who's actually running a place, and you'll know exactly what you're getting.

## Tone rules
- **Plain first.** Short sentences. One idea per sentence. No fluff.
- **Warm but honest.** "No beds free right now." "This is a room, not a resort." No over-selling.
- **Indian English, natural.** Not translated. Not corporate. The rhythm is real.
- **Playful but restrained.** A dry smile, never a joke. "Ten beds for ten strangers who'll mostly be strangers — we'll fix that."
- **No filler, no brand-bleat.** "Prajna" appears naturally. It is not used as a buzzword.

## Banned words (from the GEO/AEO reading list)
delve, surge, paramount, vital, deploy, synthesize, tapestry, bolster, synergy, realm, holistic, revolutionize, game-changing, at its core, testament, navigate the complex, multi-faceted, pivotal moment.

Replace with plain verbs and concrete nouns.

## Sentence & paragraph rules
- Max ~15 words on average. One idea per sentence.
- Headings answer the reader's next question.
- Whitespace is a friend. One idea per block.
- Numbers land first: "Four twin-sharing rooms, two private rooms."

## Schema-safe
- Factual present tense. No unverifiable claims. No absolute language where a fact is unknown.
- Every claim must be true and current: prices, beds, policies, address.

## Accent & playfulness (the "tinge")
- Short, confident. Occasional crisp aside.
- Examples: "Rooms fill fast in March and June." "We sleep, they work." "Clear the fridge? We'll tell you when."
- Tone is warm, never slangy. Never cheapens the house.
